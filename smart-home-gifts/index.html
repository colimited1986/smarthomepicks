1. Smart Plug with Energy Monitoring (Best value)

Best first smart device

Before you buy a smart home gift

  • Check Wi-Fi. Many smart devices need a stable home network, and some only work on certain Wi-Fi bands, so look at the listing.
  • Match the voice assistant. Look for Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home support, depending on what the recipient uses.
  • Watch for subscriptions. Cameras and doorbells may charge for stored video.
  • Think about privacy. For cameras, use a strong password, turn on two-factor sign-in if offered, and keep the device updated.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best first smart home device?

A smart plug. It is inexpensive, easy to set up, and useful right away for lamps, fans and small appliances.

Do smart home gadgets work without Wi-Fi?

Most need Wi-Fi for remote control and schedules. Some basic functions may keep working without it, but the details vary by device.

Are smart home devices safe to use?

They can be, if you use strong passwords, turn on two-factor sign-in where offered, and install updates. Buy from reputable sellers and read the listing for security details.
