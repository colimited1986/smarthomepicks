Four things to verify before checkout

1. What “16GB RAM” means

Confirm whether the advertised memory figure includes virtual or memory-extension technology in addition to physical RAM. The distinction can matter when comparing performance between tablets.

2. Screen resolution and brightness

The title advertises a 2K screen. Check the detailed specifications and customer feedback for resolution, brightness, viewing angles, and how comfortable it is for your usual environment.

3. Storage expansion

The listing advertises 256GB ROM and TF expansion up to 1TB. Confirm the supported card format and maximum capacity, and remember that a memory card may be sold separately.

4. Gemini AI and accessories

Check exactly how Gemini AI is accessed, whether account or network requirements apply, and which charger, case, keyboard, or other accessories are included. Do not assume every pictured item comes in the box.
