What matters most before you check out

1. Capacity and kitchen space

Capacity sounds useful only if the appliance fits your counter and the food you typically prepare. Check the product dimensions and leave the clearance recommended by the manufacturer.

2. The functions you’ll actually use

“10-in-1” is a useful starting point, but the specific modes matter more than the number. Review the current listing or manual to confirm each function you care about.

3. Cleaning and accessories

Look for the included accessories, which parts are dishwasher-safe, and the recommended cleaning process. Do not assume every removable part can go in the dishwasher.

4. Seller, returns, and warranty

Before ordering, review who sells and ships the item, delivery timing, return eligibility, and warranty terms for your purchase.
