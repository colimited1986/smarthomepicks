Questions to answer before buying

Is 128GB enough for an iPad?

It can be sufficient for everyday apps, browsing, reading, and streaming. If you store many large games, videos, photos, or creative projects locally, compare your expected usage with the available storage options.

Can it replace a laptop?

It may cover many workflows built around compatible apps, documents, browsing, and communication. If you need desktop-only software, specialized peripherals, or a traditional desktop workflow, check those requirements before replacing a laptop.
