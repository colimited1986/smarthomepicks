What to know before adding it to your routine

Tea tree and sensitive skin: tea-tree ingredients are common in skincare for blemish-prone routines, but they can irritate some people. “For sensitive skin” doesn’t mean every person will tolerate every formula.

Check the full ingredient list: the title highlights tea tree and lactobacillus, but the complete formula matters. Review the current listing and packaging for ingredients your skin may not tolerate.

Patch test first: test cautiously before regular use and stop if you notice persistent burning, redness, swelling, or worsening irritation. Avoid broken skin and the eye area unless directions explicitly allow it.

Keep expectations realistic: toner pads are not a guaranteed acne treatment. Persistent, painful, or scarring acne is worth discussing with a qualified healthcare professional.

A sensible buying check: if you already use exfoliating acids, retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, or other active ingredients, consider whether adding another product could irritate your skin. More skincare isn’t always better.
