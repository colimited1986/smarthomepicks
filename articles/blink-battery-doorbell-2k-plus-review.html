“Without Subscription” — Check This Before You Buy

This is the most important detail to verify if avoiding monthly fees is a deciding factor. A device may offer some functions without a plan while other recording, history, or smart features have separate requirements.

Confirm the no-plan experience

  • Which features are available without a paid subscription?
  • Can you save and review video clips without a plan? If so, what storage method or accessory is required?
  • Are live viewing, motion alerts, event history, and downloads handled differently?
  • Are there trial periods or renewal terms to understand?

Check your home and phone setup

  • Confirm Wi-Fi requirements and signal strength at the front door.
  • Review battery type, included batteries, expected battery life conditions, and replacement process.
  • Check mounting dimensions, weather suitability, and whether the included hardware fits your doorway.
  • Confirm app and phone compatibility before ordering.
