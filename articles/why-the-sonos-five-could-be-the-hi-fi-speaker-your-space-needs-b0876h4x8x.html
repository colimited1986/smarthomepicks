What Catches My Ear First: Audio Performance

For me, sound quality is non-negotiable with any speaker, especially something as an investment as the Sonos Five. It features six drivers—three tweeters and three mid-woofers—which are designed to handle everything from crisp highs to deep bass without distortion. The spacious stereo soundstage it promises means you can position it horizontally to really get that full stereo effect in a single speaker.

  • Six-driver design for detailed audio reproduction
  • Trueplay tuning technology adapts sound to your room’s acoustics
  • Capacitive touch controls for intuitive volume and playback adjustments

Setting It Up and Living with It

Installation simplicity is definitely something I value in any smart home tech. With the Sonos Five, setup is mostly wireless via Wi-Fi, though there is an Ethernet port if you prefer a wired connection for stability. It also has a 3.5mm audio line-in jack, so you can connect older devices like turntables or CD players if you want to blend traditional and streaming audio.

The speaker is on the larger side, weighing around 14 pounds and measuring roughly 14 by 8 by 6 inches. That means you’ll want to make sure you have the kind of space that accommodates it comfortably—not tucked away in a tiny corner—if you want the best sound.

I’d also be mindful that while it's moisture resistant—meaning it can handle a bit of humidity—it’s not designed for outdoor use or environments with a lot of moisture.

  • Wi-Fi connectivity with optional Ethernet for stable network connection
  • 3.5mm audio line-in lets you plug in analog sources
  • Large, heavy form factor requires a dedicated spot in your room

How It Plays with Your Smart Home System

Compatibility matters; I’d check if the Sonos Five works with your smart ecosystem. It supports Apple AirPlay 2, which is great if you use Apple devices. For voice assistants, it works best within the Sonos ecosystem, which might require pairing it with other compatible speakers if you want full smart assistant functionality.

You won’t find Bluetooth playback here, so if you want to connect directly from a phone without Wi-Fi, that could be a limitation for some. But for streaming high-resolution audio over Wi-Fi, it's designed to shine. The Sonos app lets you manage music across multiple rooms if you have other Sonos speakers, so it scales nicely if you want to expand later.

  • Supports Apple AirPlay 2 for convenient streaming
  • Best integrated within the Sonos multi-room ecosystem
  • No Bluetooth audio playback; Wi-Fi streaming only

Frequently asked questions

Does the Sonos Five support Bluetooth for connecting to my phone?

No, the Sonos Five does not support Bluetooth audio playback. It connects primarily via Wi-Fi and also has a 3.5mm line-in for analog sources.

Can I use the Sonos Five outdoors or in a bathroom?

The speaker is moisture resistant and can handle humid environments like a bathroom with a running shower, but it’s not waterproof or designed for outdoor use, so you should avoid exposing it to heavy moisture.

Is the Sonos Five compatible with voice assistants like Alexa or Google Assistant?

It works best within the Sonos ecosystem, which integrates with some voice assistants, but you may need additional Sonos devices or setups depending on your preferences. Check for the latest compatibility options for your voice assistant of choice.
