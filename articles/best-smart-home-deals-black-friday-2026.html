Smart-home categories to watch for Black Friday

1. Smart speakers and displays

These can serve as voice-control centers for compatible lights, plugs, sensors, and routines. Before buying, compare the assistant you use, audio quality, screen size (if applicable), and compatibility with your existing devices.

2. Smart plugs and lighting

Multi-packs can offer better per-unit value than single plugs or bulbs. Check Wi-Fi requirements, hub requirements, brightness, color options, and whether the products work with your preferred ecosystem.

3. Indoor security cameras and doorbells

Compare video quality, night vision, motion alerts, local storage, and subscription requirements. A low upfront price may be less attractive if essential recording features require a recurring fee.

4. Video doorbells and smart locks

Confirm compatibility with your door, existing wiring, lock type, and smart-home platform. For locks, consider physical-key backup, battery alerts, and who needs access.

5. Thermostats and climate controls

Compatibility is critical. Check your HVAC system, wiring, and whether a common wire (C-wire) or adapter is needed. A discount is not worth it if the model does not support your heating and cooling setup.

6. Mesh Wi-Fi and networking gear

Connected devices work best with reliable coverage. Compare coverage claims, wired ports, supported Wi-Fi standards, and whether a two- or three-unit kit fits the size and layout of your home.

How to tell whether a Black Friday deal is good

  • Compare the final price: include shipping, taxes where applicable, and any required accessories.
  • Check price history where available: a crossed-out list price alone does not prove a meaningful discount.
  • Compare exact model numbers: similarly named products may have different features or generations.
  • Read recent reviews: focus on reliability, app quality, setup, and customer support—not only star ratings.
  • Factor in subscriptions: estimate the total cost over one or two years if cloud storage or premium features are needed.
  • Check returns and warranty: especially for refurbished, open-box, or third-party marketplace listings.

When should you shop?

Retailers may launch promotions in the weeks before November 27, then change offers during Black Friday weekend and Cyber Monday on November 30, 2026. Some deals sell out or are replaced with similar models. If a product meets your needs at a price you have independently judged to be good, do not assume the same offer will remain available later.

Smart-home Black Friday checklist

  • Confirm the device works with your current ecosystem.
  • Check Wi-Fi band, hub, wiring, and regional compatibility requirements.
  • Verify whether local recording or core features require a subscription.
  • Compare the exact model and total cost with at least one alternative.
  • Read the return policy and warranty terms.
  • Recheck price and stock immediately before checkout.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday follows on November 30, 2026.

Are smart-home products always cheapest on Black Friday?

Not necessarily. Some products may see similar promotions at other times. Compare price history and competing models rather than assuming every Black Friday label represents the best price.

Should I buy a bundle?

A bundle can be good value if you will use every item and all components are compatible. Compare the bundle’s total price with the cost of buying the items separately.

Bottom line

For Black Friday 2026, prioritize compatibility and total ownership cost over the largest advertised discount. Smart plugs, lighting, cameras, speakers, locks, thermostats, and mesh Wi-Fi are useful categories to compare—but confirm current prices, stock, and terms before you publish or purchase.
