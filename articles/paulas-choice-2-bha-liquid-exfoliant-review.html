What does 2% BHA actually mean?

BHA stands for beta hydroxy acid. In skincare, salicylic acid is a commonly used BHA and is oil-soluble, which is one reason it is often included in products aimed at exfoliation and the appearance of congested pores. The 2% concentration is a useful detail when comparing formulas, but it does not tell you by itself how your skin will respond.

Because this is a leave-on liquid exfoliant, it is intended to remain on the skin rather than function as a wash-off product. That can make it a practical format for someone who wants an exfoliating step in an existing routine, provided the formula and directions suit their skin.
