Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes the standards we hold our product notes to.

Independence

We earn commissions when readers buy through our Amazon links. A commission never changes the price you pay, which products we feature or how we describe them. We don’t accept payment for placement.

Accuracy

  • We match each pick’s name to its Amazon listing title and show the product name as the listing gives it, without adding a brand when the listing doesn’t show one.
  • Product notes are based on listing and manufacturer information. Where a detail is unclear or depends on your setup, we say so and tell you what to check.
  • We don’t state specifications we can’t support.

What we don’t claim

  • We don’t claim to have tested the products we feature.
  • We don’t publish star ratings, review counts or customer testimonials.
  • We don’t show prices or discounts. Check the Amazon listing for the current price.

Price levels

Labels such as Budget-friendly, Mid-range and Premium are a rough guide to where a pick sits compared with other picks in its category. They are not prices.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake, tell us through the Contact page. We’ll review it and correct the page when the error is confirmed.
