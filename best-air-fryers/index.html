Detailed comparison: how the three differ

Ninja FO101 vs. Chefman 4-Quart: size and flexibility

The Ninja is an oven with a basket, and the Chefman is a basket. That one difference drives most of the others. The Ninja’s 5-quart air fry basket is already larger than the Chefman’s entire 4-quart capacity, and its 20.3-quart oven adds room for a sheet pan and rack cooking. The Chefman wins on footprint and simplicity: it takes far less counter space and it’s built around presets rather than a long function list.

Counter space and storage

Choose by where the appliance will live. The Chefman is the easiest to tuck away between uses. The Ninja’s listed dimensions make it a fixture that needs its own spot, with room for the French doors to open. The SPRX’s outside dimensions weren’t something we could verify, so check them before you assume it fits under a cabinet.

Cooking flexibility

The Ninja lists 10 functions, so it’s the widest-ranging of the three. The SPRX is described as an air fryer and toaster oven, which suggests a similar oven-style approach at a smaller size, though its full function list needs confirming. The Chefman focuses on air frying with four presets and a Hi-Fry mode.

Cleaning and accessories

The Chefman’s basket is described as nonstick and dishwasher safe, and the Ninja’s listing mentions dishwasher-safe accessories. We couldn’t confirm cleaning details for the SPRX. Whichever you choose, read the care instructions, since not every part of an air fryer is dishwasher safe.

What to check before buying

  1. Measure your space. Check width, depth and height, and leave clearance around vents. For the Ninja, also allow for the doors to swing open.
  2. Match capacity to your household. Think about how many people you cook for and how often you cook in batches.
  3. Confirm the exact model. Similar models and colors exist, such as other Ninja French-door versions, so match the model number on the listing.
  4. See what’s in the box. Look for the baskets, racks and pans included.
  5. Read the cleaning notes. Check which parts are dishwasher safe and how to clean the rest.
  6. Check power and outlet needs. Look at the wattage on the listing and the plug requirements.
  7. Review warranty and return terms on the Amazon listing.

Important buying considerations

Capacity numbers aren’t apples to apples. A basket’s quarts and an oven’s quarts measure different things. The Ninja’s 20.3 quarts describes the oven cavity, and its air fry basket is 5 quarts. Compare the part you’ll actually cook in.

Bigger isn’t always better. A large oven can take longer to find room for, and it may be more than you need for weeknight dinners for two. Pick the size you’ll use often.

FAQ

How many people does a 4-quart air fryer serve?

Listing information for the Chefman describes about two to four servings. Appetites and recipes vary, so larger meals may need more than one batch.

Is an air fryer toaster oven better than a basket air fryer?

It depends on how you cook. An oven-style model usually offers more room and more functions, while a basket model is typically more compact and simpler. Space and household size are the usual deciders.

Why does the Ninja FO101 list two capacities?

The 20.3-quart figure is for the oven. The air fry basket inside is 5 quarts. Use the number that matches how you plan to cook.
