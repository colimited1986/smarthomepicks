Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information HomeFit Picks collects and what happens when you click links to other sites.

Information we collect

HomeFit Picks does not have accounts, comments, checkout or sign-up forms, and we don’t ask you for personal information. The search box and filters on our pages run in your browser, and what you type is not sent to us.

Cookies, analytics and ads

The code of this site does not include analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and the site does not set cookies of its own. If we add any of these in the future, we will update this page first.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses and browser details when you load a page. We don’t control that collection. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Links to Amazon and other sites

Product links take you to Amazon.com. When you click one, Amazon may use cookies or similar technology to record that you came from our site, which is how we receive a commission on qualifying purchases. Amazon’s use of your information is governed by its own privacy notice, not this one.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Questions
