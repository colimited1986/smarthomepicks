How to choose a gift under $50

  • Match the gift to a habit. Someone who cooks often will use a chopper. Someone who bakes will use a scale.
  • Pick practical over novel. Useful gifts get used for years, while gimmicks end up in a drawer.
  • Check compatibility for smart gifts. Ask which voice assistant they use, or include a gift receipt.
  • Look at the return window on Amazon in case the gift is not quite right.
