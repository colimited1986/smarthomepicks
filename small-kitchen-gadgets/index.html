How to decide what earns counter space

  • Store flat or store away. A gadget that fits in a drawer or cabinet is easier to live with.
  • Choose multi-use over single-use. One appliance that replaces two is the best deal for a small kitchen.
  • Be honest about frequency. If you would not use it at least weekly, it will end up in the way.
  • Leave clearance. Appliances that vent steam or heat need open space around them and should not sit under low cabinets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What small kitchen gadgets should I buy first?

Start with the cheap, compact ones: a vegetable chopper and a kitchen scale. They save time right away and barely take up room.

Can one appliance replace several?

A multi-cooker can replace a slow cooker, a pressure cooker and a rice cooker for many households, though it will not do everything perfectly.

How much counter space does an air fryer oven need?

It varies by model, so check the dimensions in the listing and leave open space around it for airflow.
